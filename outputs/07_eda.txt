----- 7.8 Initial insights -----
1. Sex is the strongest predictor of survival: ~74% of females survived vs ~19% of males.
2. Pclass shows a clear gradient: Class1 ~62%, Class2 ~47%, Class3 ~24% survival.
3. Passengers embarking at Cherbourg (C) had the highest survival rate (~55%),
   largely because a higher proportion travelled in Class 1.
4. Fare is positively correlated with survival; Age is weakly correlated.
5. HasCabin (proxy for Class 1/2) correlates positively with survival.
6. Family size (SibSp + Parch): travelling alone or with very large families
   both reduced survival odds - a useful engineered feature for later modelling.
